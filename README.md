#Boberto Sanchez & Co.
_Boberto Sanchez & Co._ is a simple logic game wherein you walk from one side to the next and gather information from your friends about their preferred seating arrangement. 

##Engine
_Boberto Sanchez & Co._ is made with Godot. 

##Where is the final build?
The final build of _Boberto Sanchez & Co._ is available through the "latest branch" branch, available through the branches section. 

##How to play?
You can either choose to download the project and open it in Godot to modify it, or you can play the game directly through the Itch.io link below

https://the-gin-corner.itch.io/boberto
